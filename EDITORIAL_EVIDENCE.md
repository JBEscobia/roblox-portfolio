## Direct source reads (2026-09-10)

Gravity Dash projectile systems and AniPal shared shops were requested after the audits, which mention them only in passing. Roblox Studio was not running, so the MCP bridge could not reach either place. Both places had Studio auto-recovery files in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Roblox\RobloxStudio\AutoSaves\`: Gravity Dash `76443130781048_AutoRecovery_17.rbxl` (2026-09-08 07:03, the day of the audit) and AniPal `77853585138886_AutoRecovery_10.rbxl` (2026-08-14 16:02). A read-only decoder for the binary place format (LZ4 chunks; INST, PROP and PRNT records) extracted every script with its full instance path.

The decodes match the audits' own inventories: 326 scripts for Gravity Dash, as Pass 1 counted, and 363 for AniPal, with 148,447 lines against the audit's 148,674 on 2026-09-08. Nothing was written to either file or to the places. Findings describe those snapshots; later edits in Studio could differ, and none of this is a runtime test.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Gravity Dash trading remains disabled in the audit. Do not turn its technical architecture into a live feature claim.
- Time Tag has a usable currency-spend Trail shop path. Its separately granted round-reward `TrailCrates` have no discovered consumption path; do not conflate them. Enchantment movement effects remain disconnected. Trade dependencies remain prototype technical material.
- AniPal's mechanics map reports no registered developer products; it is not the monetization example. Since 2026-09-10 its shared shop rotation is promoted on the strength of the direct source read above. Fish sales and bait purchases use the in-game Doubloon currency only and are not presented as monetization.
- AniPal Guard, invasion, housing, hidden abilities, and unhooked weather modifier tables are not promoted. Catalog clothing is an optional, verified-combination follow-up, not a normal acquisition claim.
- Historical performance commentary is not a benchmark. Diagnostic captures, if later requested, must be real; none are required to understand the gameplay portfolio.
